Privacy policy

Personal Research Backup is operated by AmolAdhav for personal research-file backup and recovery. This policy covers the local application and this public information website.

Information used by the application

The owner selects local research files for backup. Restic encrypts their contents and repository metadata on the owner's computer, and rclone transfers the encrypted repository to the owner's Google Drive. Restore operations retrieve those encrypted files for local decryption.

The application uses Google OAuth client credentials and authorization tokens to access Drive without receiving the owner's Google password. These credentials are stored in protected configuration on the owner's computer, outside the public website and source repository.

Google permissions and purpose

The requested https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope permits management of files created by, or explicitly authorized for, the application. Access is used to create, list, read, update and, when directed by the owner, delete backup repository files. It is not used for advertising, profiling or model training.

The application does not sell Google user data or share it with advertisers. Google receives the encrypted backup files and the account and service information needed to operate Drive. Encryption does not conceal all service metadata, such as account identity, upload times or storage usage.

Retention, deletion and revocation

The owner controls backup retention. No automatic deletion policy or nightly backup schedule is currently active. Backup snapshots remain until the owner removes them through restic, or deletes the repository from Drive. Drive trash and provider retention rules may also apply.

The owner may revoke access through Google Account third-party connections and remove local rclone credentials. Revoking access stops future authorized access; it does not delete existing backup files. Existing snapshots require separate deletion. Deleting the repository password can also make recovery impossible.

This website

These static pages contain no application analytics, advertising, forms, cookies or JavaScript. They do not collect Google credentials or research files. There is no public backup account registration.

The intended host is GitHub Pages. When hosted there, GitHub may process visitor information such as IP addresses and request logs under its own privacy statement. Visiting external links is subject to the destination's practices.

Security and limits

The workflow uses local encryption, protected credential storage and HTTPS connections. The owner is responsible for device access, password recovery, account security and restore testing. These measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee protection against every compromise, deletion or loss.

Configuration is still in progress. A successful backup, tested restore and unattended operation have not yet been confirmed.

Contact and policy changes

The owner is AmolAdhav on GitHub. See that profile for available contact options. Do not post passwords, tokens or private data publicly. This policy will be updated when the application's data practices change; the date above identifies the latest revision.
